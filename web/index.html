Reclaimer: get back your precious disk space

    
Reclaimer isn't an app uninstaller or a general disk cleaner. It knows the caches, build output and
       tooling leftovers a developer's Mac accumulates, sorts them by how safe they are to remove, and lets
       you pick item by item. Nothing is deleted without a confirmation, and admin-only paths go through
       macOS's own password dialog.
